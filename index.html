Tank Frontier - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Tank Frontier ("the game") is made by Pixeloop Studios. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

We do not collect personal data

The game works fully offline. It does not ask for an account, name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (missions completed, medals, upgrades, achievements) and your settings (language, volume, text size, controls) are saved only on your own device. A random identifier is created on the device for the save file; it never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or using "Reset progress" in Settings, deletes this data.

No ads, no tracking, no purchases

The game contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no third-party sign-in and no in-app purchases.

Permissions

The game only uses the vibration permission, for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for children. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects no personal data from children.
